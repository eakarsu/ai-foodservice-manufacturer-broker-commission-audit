# Foodservice Manufacturer Broker Commission Audit

Audit broker commissions against territories, products, customers, net sales, exclusions, and contractual rates.

**Primary buyer:** Food manufacturers and broker networks. **Evidence:** broker agreements, territories, customers, distributor depletion, net sales, exclusions, commission rates, draws, statements, and payments.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Broker agreement library
- Territory customer mapping
- Product eligibility registry
- Distributor depletion ingestion
- Gross sales calculation
- Returns deduction
- Promotion exclusion
- Net sales calculation
- Commission tier calculation
- Draw recovery control
- Statement reconciliation
- Duplicate commission detection
- Broker dispute workflow
- Payment correction
- Broker territory analytics

Run `./start.sh`, then open <http://127.0.0.1:4707>. API: `5707`.
